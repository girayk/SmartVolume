# SmartVolume Privacy Policy

SmartVolume doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

- There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
- The app talks only to your Samsung Smart Monitor on your local network, to find it and to send it volume commands. It contacts no other server.
- The monitor's pairing key is kept in your Mac's Keychain, and the app's settings are kept on your Mac. They are only used to talk to your monitor.
- The Input Monitoring permission is used only to see the Volume Up, Volume Down and Mute keys. Key presses are not recorded or stored.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/girayk/SmartVolume/issues.
